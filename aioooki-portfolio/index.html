05 / The studio Independent by design

Character studio.
Obsessive craft.

AIOOOKI is an independent 3D character practice sculpting iconic figures, synthetic creatures and stylized protagonists for games, cinematics, collectibles and virtual fashion.

Fewer projects, deeper worlds. I work directly with game directors, lead character artists and creative leads from initial anatomical silhouette to final engine lookdev — no middle layer, zero diluted artistic intent.

Silhouette first. Always. Then the tactile micro-surfaces.

Every character starts with gesture and weight. What is their physical posture? How do their proportions read at a glance? We find the anatomical tension, sculpt bold silhouettes in ZBrush, and author PBR shaders that react with visceral realism under any lighting condition.

  • AHigh-resolution ZBrush sculpts with distinct silhouettes
  • BMulti-layer PBR lookdev, SSS skin & tactile fabrics
  • CClean quad retopology optimized for rigging & UE5

Character PipelineFrom raw sketch to game engine
  1. 01

    Silhouette

    Nail the proportions, weight and attitude in 2D/3D blockouts before touching high-frequency detail.

  2. 02

    Sculpt

    Carve muscle rhythm, anatomical nuance, cloth tension and facial expression in ZBrush.

  3. 03

    Topology

    Lay down clean deformation loops, unwrap distortion-free UVs, and bake normal & cavity maps.

  4. 04

    Lookdev

    Surface with tactile PBR textures, set up engine lighting turntables, and deliver production assets.
